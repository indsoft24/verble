English learning app description
The intent
Intent is to motivate the users by instilling confidence and by grooming their personality. The demand of knowing English is growing rapidly. However, the friends in India need a lot local work to be done to improve their English. With English, becoming more popular and demanding, people are left with no choice, but to learn English as much as they can. I understand, the people living in urban cities are encouraged and empowered enough by the teachers and parents to learn English, however, from rural territories, still need some work to be done. 
My intent is to educated as many people as much. I can buy, sparing time out of my available schedule.
In the era of internet and now, AI, there is almost no limit in the amount of information available. People do have access to internet, Instagram, GenAI tools like ChatGPT Gemini, and many other such platforms. However, there is so much of the information that it becomes almost impossible to keep Trek of it, pursue and retain the memory. Because we consume so much on daily basis. This is too far the English learning platforms as well and for any learning platform for that matter. There is no structure, there is no format, there is no tracking, and there is no support if you’re doing anything wrong. 
In today’s time, you can’t just say ‘let it be’, because not knowing English is costing you as much that you can’t even calculate. You’re missing on opportunities, you’re missing and confidence, and eventually start under estimating your potential. You just can’t afford to tell someone that you’re not competent enough because you lack the command in English.
With the help of this course, we are helping you achieve what you’re true potential is. Whether you are job seeker or a student or a housewife, a small business owner for someone who is aspiring to go to different country. Our course makes you 0 to hero in a attract approach and in structured manner.

The Concept
	•	App has the unrestricted content as well as the premium content, which is available on making a payment.
	•	Unrestricted content is bifurcated into Free, Bronze, Silver
	•	By default, only free content is unlocked, bronze and silver are locked.
	•	User accesses the free content for 30 days in a row without skipping, then they’ll be promoted to access the bronze content.
	•	If in last 30 days, user attempted the free content for 70% of the days they will be presented with the bronze content.
	•	User then access the bronze content for another 60 days before they are promoted to the silver content.
	•	If in last 60 days, user attempted the free content for 70% of the days they will be presented with the bronze content.
	•	Once they unlock the silver content, they can continue to access the free content for 90 days.
	•	After 90 days based on the daily access of the app and the free content, they will be rewarded another 90 days.
	•	If in last 90 days, user attempted the free content for 70% of the days they will be presented with the bronze content.
	•	
	•	Paid content is bifurcated into Gold, full course, Bonus content
	•	With Gold subscription, they can get access to the gold content for 01 year. 
	•	Gold subscription also opens the free, bronze, silver content.
	•	Full course is the main highlight of the course.
	•	With full course, they get access to everything plus Gold plus bonus content
	•	They get access to the full course and the remaining content for one year.
	•	After one year, they continue to have the access to the full course, while the other sections from free to gold will get locked, and they will have to buy gold separately as per the above model.
	•	Full course is actually a complete package of full structured, level wise English learning journey.
	•	The full course is divided into multiple modules
	•	Each module has multiple videos. By default, only one video is unlocked while others are locked. Each video need to be completed in order to unlock the next video in the same module. 
	•	Some users can mark videos complete manually as they may feel they understand the topic already.
	•	Each module has puzzles/quiz.
	•	By default, only first module unlocked. User completes the module along with the puzzle with passing marks to unlock the next module. 70% score is the passing mark to unlock the next module.
	•	At the end of all the modules and all the puzzles, users has the option to appear for the certificate assessment that includes 150-200 questions. This includes the subjective as well as objective questions.
	•	After scoring 70% in the final assessment, they get the E certificate.
	•	All the content type has have an identification colour as well as an icon.

	•	I am able to manage most of the things from the dedicated backend less than the database so that I don’t have to get the Code updated on the front and every now and then.
	•	Updating the front and case of the app is composing and require a lot of logistical arrangement, and not just the technical efforts.
	•	The app also has the help section. In this section, I am able to add various guidelines, and supporting document for and users to refer. For example, how to create a Gmail account, how to access particular section in the, how to register on ChatGPT/Gemini.
